Niacinamide is one of the few brightening and barrier ingredients that earns a place on melanin-rich South African faces without the legal and safety mess of unregulated hydroquinone. For marks that follow acne, insect bites, razor bumps and heat-flared melasma under Highveld and coastal UV, a well-formulated 5 percent used daily beats a 20 percent claim used twice.

## Quick answer

Niacinamide at 4 to 5 percent, used consistently for eight to twelve weeks, can reduce the look of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation and support a battered barrier. It does not bleach constitutive melanin. It does not replace tinted SPF. Pair it with photoprotection that covers UVA1 and visible light or you will treat and retrigger in the same week.

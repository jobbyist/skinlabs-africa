## Quick answer

Oral isotretinoin shrinks sebaceous output. Cheilitis, tight cheeks and cracked corners of the mouth are expected pharmacology, not a sign the dose is working harder. In South Africa the same month also brings hard water, indoor heaters or coastal wind, and UV high enough that a raw barrier collects pigment. Soften the water contact, drop the foam, occlude the lips on a schedule, and treat tinted SPF as part of the prescription even when the packet only mentions dryness.

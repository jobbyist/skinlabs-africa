The mask is doing its job. The jawline that looks like a second puberty is the cost. South African shift work — hospitals, mines, security, retail queues, taxi ranks — keeps fabric or a respirator on the same centimetres of skin for eight to twelve hours. Heat, sweat and friction do the rest. Calling it “dirty pores” is how people add a harsh scrub to an already inflamed field.

## Quick answer

Maskne is acne mechanica plus a humid microclimate under PPE. It is not a personal hygiene failure. Reduce friction, keep the skin slightly moisturised rather than stripped, skip comedogenic layers under the mask, and treat inflammatory lesions the same way you would treat ordinary acne — slowly, with evidence, under daily SPF because South African UV turns every pimple into a brown circle.

A 2022 survey of healthcare workers in the maskne literature found facial dermatoses clustered where the device sat: cheeks, chin, nasal bridge. Cardinal Health summarised reports that more than six in ten healthcare workers described itch, rash or acne after extended wear. Those numbers were generated in pandemic wards. The mechanism did not retire when the posters came down.

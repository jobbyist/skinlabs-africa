The face gets the bottle. The parting gets the Highveld. Hair is not a hat. A centre part, a faded sides-and-top, a shaved line, or a thinning crown is a strip of scalp aimed at South African UV for hours, and Isaacs and colleagues’ 2025 sun-protection review for this country treats photoprotection as a whole-person problem, not a cheek-only ritual.

## Quick answer

Hair reduces UV that reaches scalp skin. It does not cancel it. Density, thickness and coil pattern change the dose. A visible parting, a receding hairline, traction-thinned edges and a freshly cut fade are the gaps. A brim, a scarf or doek worn off the part, a scalp SPF fluid or stick along the line, and a habit of moving the part are the intervention. Oil is not sunscreen. Shine spray is not sunscreen. A leave-in with “UV filter” in the marketing copy is rarely a measured SPF film on skin.

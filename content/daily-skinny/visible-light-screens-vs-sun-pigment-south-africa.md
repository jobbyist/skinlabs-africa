The reel said your phone is staining your cheeks. The commute is the actual lamp. Visible light from the sun is a pigmentation input on melanin-rich skin. A handset at 40 centimetres is a different dose. Confusing the two is how people buy blue light creams and still sit in a north-facing taxi without tinted SPF.

## Quick answer

Mahmoud and later groups showed that visible light, especially with UVA1, darkens skin types III to VI in a way ordinary UVB-weighted SPF does not fully block. Iron oxides in tinted sunscreens absorb a useful slice of that visible band. Screen time is not zero, but outdoor and window light dominate the budget for melasma and PIH in South Africa. Treat the sky first. Then argue about night mode.

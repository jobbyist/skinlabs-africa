Gym floors in Durban in January and packed spin rooms in Sandton in September share a climate the face never signed up for. Heat, occlusion, sweat salts and a polyester vest that stays wet through the taxi home are how backs, chests and shoulders collect acne that then leaves brown maps.

## Quick answer

Body acne in South African heat is friction plus microbes plus a film that never fully dries. Wash once after training with a short-contact benzoyl peroxide or a gentle cleanser, change the shirt before the ride home, and put water-resistant SPF on shoulders that will see sun in the parking lot.

SPF 50 that melted off at 10am is not SPF 50 at noon. South African summers pair extreme UV with sweat, swimming, school sport and commutes that sit in traffic with the window down. The product on the bottle only works if a usable film is still on your skin.

## One-sentence answer

Sweat-proof summer sunscreen in South Africa is a reapplication habit plus a formula that stays put — not a higher number on a tube you apply once.

## Quick answer

- Choose broad-spectrum SPF 30 to 50 with a UVA circle or PA+++ rating, and water/sweat resistance if you actually sweat or swim.
- Apply two fingertip units to the face and more than you think to ears, neck and hands.
- Reapply every two hours outdoors, and after swimming, towelling or a heavy sweat.
- Tinted mineral or hybrid formulas help melanin-rich skin against visible light as well as UV.
- Clothing, a hat and shade still do more work than any cream when the Highveld UV index is extreme.
- Cheap pharmacy SPF you will reapply beats prestige SPF that stays in the gym bag.
